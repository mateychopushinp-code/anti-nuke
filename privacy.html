Anti-Nuke — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled on a server, Anti-Nuke may process information provided through Discord's API, including Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, bot IDs, server configuration information, and audit log information related to security events.

Anti-Nuke does not intentionally collect Discord passwords, payment information, or Discord account tokens.

2. Security Logs

Anti-Nuke may record security-related events such as mass bans, mass kicks, channel deletions, role deletions, unauthorized bot additions, and unauthorized administrator permissions.

3. Backups

Anti-Nuke may store server configuration backups containing channel names and settings, role names and settings, role permissions, channel permission overwrites, and other configuration information required for restoration.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information processed by Anti-Nuke is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the Bot, provide its functionality, or comply with applicable law.

5. Data Retention

Security logs and backups may be retained for as long as they are needed to provide Anti-Nuke's security and restoration features.

6. Discord

Anti-Nuke operates through Discord's platform and Discord's API. Use of Discord remains subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by Anti-Nuke. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests regarding Anti-Nuke, please contact the Bot's developer through the Discord account or support method associated with Anti-Nuke.
