Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Anti-Nuke is a Discord security bot designed to help protect Discord servers from destructive and unauthorized activity.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Anti-Nuke may process when it is added to or used in a Discord server.

2. Information We May Process

To provide security features, Anti-Nuke may process certain Discord-related information.

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server IDs
  • Channel IDs and channel configuration
  • Role IDs and role configuration
  • Bot and member information needed for security actions
  • Discord audit log information related to security events

3. Security Logs

Anti-Nuke may record security-related events in order to detect suspicious activity and help server administrators investigate attacks.

These events may include mass bans, mass kicks, channel deletion, role deletion, unauthorized bot additions and unauthorized administrator permissions.

4. Server Backups

Anti-Nuke may create backups of certain server configuration information to assist with automatic restoration.

Backup information may include channel names, channel settings, role names, role settings, permissions and permission overwrites.

Backups are used for security and restoration purposes and are not intended to store private conversations or message content.

5. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Anti-Nuke does not intentionally collect Discord account passwords, payment card information or Discord account tokens.

6. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Anti-Nuke may be used to:

  • Detect suspicious server activity.
  • Respond to destructive attacks.
  • Restore supported server configuration.
  • Provide security logs to server administrators.
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of the bot.

7. Data Sharing

Anti-Nuke does not sell personal information.

Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the service, provide requested functionality or comply with applicable legal requirements.

8. Data Retention

Security logs and backups may be retained for as long as they are reasonably needed to provide security and restoration features.

Removing Anti-Nuke from a server may stop further processing of that server's information by the bot.

9. Discord

Anti-Nuke operates through Discord and is subject to the functionality and limitations of the Discord platform.

Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

10. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by Anti-Nuke. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as Anti-Nuke develops or its features change.

The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Anti-Nuke's handling of information, please use the official Discord or support method associated with the project.
