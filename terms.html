You agree to use the Bot in accordance with Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

2. Server Permissions

Anti-Nuke may require permissions such as viewing audit logs, managing channels, managing roles, banning members, kicking members, and managing webhooks.

The Bot may automatically respond to suspicious activity. This may include removing malicious users, restoring deleted channels or roles, and recording security events.

Anti-Nuke does not guarantee that every malicious action will be detected or prevented.

Backups are intended to assist with recovery and should not be considered a guaranteed or permanent copy of a Discord server.
